### Summary

The design is sound and implementable: rules BR1.2 to BR1.4 and the row layout match the git output (my re-run of the prototype gives 0 mismatches) and the widening of the slash rule cannot change the existing shapes. Two Major gaps should be fixed before approval: BR1.5 (b) needs the "node does not open a new lane" precondition (the literal text fails 484 oracle cases), and the scope predicate must be stated once, at the whole-graph level; the Minor items cover the code-change detail, the unverified wide-lane extrapolation, and the test and docs lists.
