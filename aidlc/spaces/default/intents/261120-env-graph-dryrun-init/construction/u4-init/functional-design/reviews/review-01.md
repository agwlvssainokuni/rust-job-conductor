### Summary

The design covers every FR for U4, both templates really pass `validate`, and the exit codes and the std-only, no-unsafe approach are sound. The main gap is that the error classification for `create_new` failures (directory, symlink, trailing slash, missing parent) is not specified in a way that gives the stated exit codes, plus smaller advisory points on atomic publication, OS differences for `--force`, and name echoing in usage errors.
